FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does it use the microphone or record what I hear?

No. Locutor only reads the title and artist of the song already playing, through the system. It doesn't access the microphone or record any audio. On Windows it asks for no permissions; on Android, only what Android calls «Notification access», the only way to know which song is playing (it doesn't read your messages).

I installed it and it doesn't comment. What do I do?

It's almost always one of these four steps: add your free key, grant the permission (on Android), play the music on the same device (not on a speaker or TV) and tap ▶ on the cover. We explain it in a 2-minute video, with the most common messages and what to do with each.

Do I need an AI key? Is it hard?

Yes, your own — and it's easy: paste your key and the app recognizes it. There are free, no-card options: Gemini (aistudio.google.com), Groq (console.groq.com), Mistral, Cerebras, OpenRouter, Hugging Face, SiliconFlow or SEA-LION. OpenAI, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, Sarvam, Solar, PLaMo or Sabiá also work. You can save several: if one runs out of quota, the app switches to another.

Where does it get its facts?

Locutor works like a well-researched search: before commenting, it checks trusted sources —Genius, Wikipedia, MusicBrainz, Last.fm and iTunes— and relies on them. Each card shows where each fact comes from.

Does it work with free Spotify? And with my country's app?

Yes. It works with Spotify (free or Premium), YouTube Music, Apple Music, Deezer, Amazon Music, JioSaavn, Gaana, QQ Music, NetEase, Anghami, Boomplay, Yandex Music, Melon, KKBOX, Palco MP3… and almost any app that shows the song playing on Windows or Android. Locutor only needs to know what's playing.

Does it comment on podcasts or videos?

No: your host comments on songs. If a podcast is playing, it tells you and doesn't comment on it like a song.

Which voices can I use?

Several, and you choose one per host: your own AI's voice (OpenAI or Gemini; with a free Gemini key it already sounds very natural), a free natural voice or voices with your key from Microsoft Azure (400 voices in 140 languages), Google Cloud, Deepgram, Sarvam, Cartesia or ElevenLabs. If the free voice isn't available at that moment, Locutor uses an alternative or leaves the comment written on screen.

What does Premium include?

Premium (€4.99/mo) unlocks the long, in-depth comment, premium voices and the music quiz without limits, and removes the daily cap. The AI is still your key; Premium pays for the app's advanced modes.

And on Android?

Yes. Locutor is already on Google Play for Android, and on Microsoft Store for Windows 10 and 11.

Do you store my key?

Not on our server. Your key is stored encrypted on your own device and used directly with your AI provider. You can delete it, along with your history, from Settings → Delete my data. No ads and no selling data.
